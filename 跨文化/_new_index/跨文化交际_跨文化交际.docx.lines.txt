DOC:L70: culture 的层次：(the three layer of culture)

DOC:L72: Q: What is the body of cultural traditions?
DOC:L73: They are referring to the shared language, traditions, and beliefs that set each of these peoples apart from others. 他们指的是使每个民族与众不同的共同语言，传统和信仰。
DOC:L74: (2)second layer: subculture (亚文化，次文化)
DOC:L75: Q: Can you give some examples to illustrate subculture?
DOC:L76: Examples of easily identifiable subcultures in the United States include ethnic groups such as Vietnamese Americans, African Americans，and Mexican Americans. Members of each of these subcultures share a common identity, food tradition, dialect or language, and other cultural traits that come from their common ancestral background and experience. 在美国，易于识别的亚文化的例子包括越南裔美国人，非裔美国人和墨西哥裔美国人等族裔群体。这些亚文化的每个成员都具有共同的身份，饮食传统，方言或语言，以及来自其共同祖先背景和经验的其他文化特征。

DOC:L131: 第二章
DOC:L132: Intercultural communication
DOC:L133: 2.1 Intercultural communication的定义：
DOC:L134: Intercultural communication is communication between members of different cultures. It involves interaction between people whose cultural perceptions and symbol systems differ enough to influence the communication event.
DOC:L135: 跨文化交流是不同文化成员之间的交流。它涉及人与人之间的互动，这些人的文化观念和符号系统差异足以影响交流事件。
DOC:L136: 跨文化交流的形式：
DOC:L137: (1)International Communication 国际交流
DOC:L138: International communication takes place between nations and governments rather than individuals; It is quite formal and ritualized(仪式化）
DOC:L139: (2)Interethnic Communication 民族间的交流
DOC:L140: Interethnic communication refers to communication between people of the same race but different ethnic backgrounds.
DOC:L141: (3)Interracial Communication 跨种族沟通
DOC:L142: Interracial communication occurs when the source and the receiver exchanging messages are from different races which are related to physical characteristics.
DOC:L143: (4)Interregional Communication 区域间交流
DOC:L144: Interregional communication refers to the exchange of messages between members of the dominant culture within a country.
DOC:L145: 2.3 形成跨文化交流的原因：
DOC:L146: (1) Technological development:
DOC:L147: 1)the improvement of information technology
DOC:L148: 2)modern communication and transportation networks
DOC:L149: (2)Globalization of the economy: multinational corporations 跨国公司
DOC:L150: (3)Widespread population migration: multiculturalism 多元文化主义
DOC:L151: 2.4为什么要学习跨文化？
DOC:L152: (1) Acquiring the knowledge and skills of intercultural communication is a priority to people in becoming a global citizen. 获得跨文化交流的知识和技能是人们成为全球公民的首要任务。
DOC:L153: (2) Developing a broader worldview, a more global psychology and the cultural skills is necessary for building relationships and solving problems. 建立更广泛的世界观，更全球化的心理学和文化技能对于建立关系和解决问题是必不可少的。
DOC:L154: 学习跨文化的益处：
DOC:L155: 1）to appreciate the opportunities and challenges that each culture presents.
DOC:L156: 体会每种文化带来的机遇和挑战
DOC:L157: 2)enhance our ability to select and perform communication behaviors appropriate to various settings. 增强我们选择和执行适合各种设置的通信行为的能力
DOC:L158: 3)get to know how we became what we are and are less threatened by those from other backgrounds. 了解我们如何成为我们自己，并受到来自其他背景的人的威胁较少
DOC:L159: 4)clearly communicate the intercultural vision to others and translate this vision into practice in the diverse workplace. 清楚地将跨文化的愿景传达给他人，并将这一愿景转化为在不同工作场所中的实践。

DOC:L174: 个人身份产生于那些把你从其他群体成员中区分出来的东西，并把你标记为特殊或有些独特的东西。

DOC:L208: values
DOC:L209: Q: What is the relationship between values and culture?
DOC:L210: Values underlie the qualities and actions that people consider necessary and vital to sustain their culture. Values establish the standard for maintaining a culture. 价值观是人们认为维持其文化必要和至关重要的品质和行为的基础。
DOC:L211: Q: The significance of values
DOC:L212: They inform people what is considered right and wrong, good and bad, correct and incorrect, appropriate and inappropriate. 他们告知人们什么是对与错，好与坏，正确与错误，适当与不适当。
DOC:L213: values的定义：
DOC:L214: What you believe in becomes the foundation for your values. What you find desirable for yourself and for the society you live in is a result of your values. More precisely, “culturally shaped personal values determine how we live our lives,” and people normally think these values should be adhered to not only by themselves but also by everyone else.
DOC:L215: 你的信仰会成为你价值观的基础。你觉得你自己和你所生活的社会想要的是你的价值观的结果。更确切地说，“文化塑造的个人价值观决定了我们如何生活”，人们通常认为这些价值观不仅应该由他们自己坚持，也应该由其他人坚持。

DOC:L246: language的定义:
DOC:L247: language is a set of shared symbols or signs that a cooperative group of people has mutually agreed to use to help them create meaning.
DOC:L248: 语言是一组共同的符号或符号，一群合作的人共同同意使用这些符号或符号来帮助他们创造意义。
DOC:L249: language的功能：
DOC:L250: social interaction：用于与人沟通
DOC:L251: social cohesion：语言是一个社会的凝聚力
DOC:L252: expressions of identity：自身身份的表达
DOC:L253: characteristics of language 语言的特点：
DOC:L254: (1)language is Symbolic：语言是符号化的（而非实际的意义）
DOC:L255: Symbols—essential ingredient; words, actions, or objects that stand for or represent a unit of meaning.
DOC:L256: 符号—基本成分；代表或代表含义单位的单词，动作或对象。
DOC:L257: (2)language is Rule Governed:语言是有规则地（有语法的）
DOC:L258: Rules– governing the compositions and ordering of the symbols.
DOC:L259: 规则–控制符号的组成和顺序。
DOC:L260: (3)language is Subjective: 语言是主观的
DOC:L261: Meaning of the language resides in people, not in words.
DOC:L262: 语言的含义在于人，而不是语言。
DOC:L263: (4)language is Dynamic: 语言是动态(也会变)的
DOC:L264: Language is dynamic and has its own life cycle.
DOC:L265: Language changes on their own account with the passage of time, even in isolation. 语言会随着时间的流逝而改变，即使是孤立地也是如此。
DOC:L266: Language also changes by taking in loan words from other languages and incorporating them. 通过吸收其他语言的外来词并将其合并，语言也发生了变化。

DOC:L273: Body and facial gestures 身体和面部手势
DOC:L274: Eye contact 眼神交流

DOC:L277: The quality of speech 演讲的质量
DOC:L278: 7.2 nonverbal communication的功能：
DOC:L279: expressing internal states: 表达内心的状态:非言语交际可以用于表达想法、态度、感觉和情绪。
DOC:L280: creating identity:创立（别人在自己看来的）身份（凭感觉和经验）。
DOC:L281: regulating interaction: 规范互动，课堂上举手就是意味着有问题要问。

DOC:L283: substituting for words:代替语言。给你竖个中指你就懂他意思了。
DOC:L284: 7.3 characteristics of nonverbal communication的特点：
DOC:L285: 1) nonverbal communication is a subtle, nonlinguistic, multidimensional, and spontaneous process.
DOC:L286: 非语言交际是一种微妙的、非语言的、多维的、自发的过程。
DOC:L287: 2) nonverbal communication is less systematized and less precise than verbal communication.
