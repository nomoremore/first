DOC:L1: Q5: List ten communication characteristics that you would like to develop if you were selected to visit another country as an exchange student
DOC:L2: Language Proficiency: Mastering the local language allows for deeper connections and understanding of the culture.
DOC:L3: Active Listening: Actively listening to others demonstrates respect and helps you grasp nuances in conversations.
DOC:L4: Cultural Sensitivity: Understanding and respecting cultural differences can prevent misunderstandings and foster positive relationships.
DOC:L5: Nonverbal Communication: Being aware of and using appropriate gestures, facial expressions, and body language can aid in effective communication.
DOC:L6: Empathy: Putting yourself in others' shoes helps you understand their perspectives and communicate more effectively.
DOC:L7: Clarity and Conciseness: Expressing yourself clearly and succinctly ensures your message is understood, especially if you're communicating in a non-native language.
DOC:L8: Adaptability: Being flexible and adaptable in your communication style allows you to connect with people from diverse backgrounds.
DOC:L9: Assertiveness: Asserting your needs and opinions respectfully helps you navigate various social situations and assert your boundaries.
DOC:L10: Cross-Cultural Communication Skills: Understanding how cultural norms influence communication styles enables you to communicate effectively across cultures
DOC:L11: Conflict Resolution: Developing skills to address conflicts constructively and find mutually beneficial solutions fosters positive relationships and cultural exchange.
