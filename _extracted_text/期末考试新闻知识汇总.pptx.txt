Language of news: compound word
Compound
words are made up of two (or more) separate words. You can combine nouns (a
flatmate
), adjectives (lovesick) or verbs (jump-start) to make compound words.
Conciseness
: Compound words condense information, allowing journalists to convey meaning succinctly without lengthy explanations.
Clarity
: They can create new meanings that are easily understood, especially when combining familiar words to describe situations or concepts.
Engagement
: Creative or unique compound words can intrigue readers and make headlines more memorable.

What is a Feature?
A feature story is all about the details: painting a picture of a person, event, or place on the written page so your topic becomes alive to the reader. They allow a writer to be creative. It's not just about facts; feature stories are more about storytelling and weaving an interesting narrative.
特写报道重在细节刻画：以文字为笔墨，生动勾勒人物、事件与场景，让报道主题鲜活立体地呈现在读者眼前。这类文体给予写作者充分的创作空间，内容不局限于客观事实；相较于纯信息陈述，特写报道更侧重故事铺陈与趣味叙事的融合打磨。

Definitions of Features
A news feature is a type of feature story written in the style of a news article.
It tackles a topic with painstaking detail and requires
facts and research
to back the story.
News features are increasingly found in publications, as they are
more engaging
for readers than hard news. To write a news feature, journalists must focus on
a specific topic
that can be thoroughly covered within a given word count
（字数上限）
. The topic must also be important and include firsthand accounts of real people, opinions from experts and facts and evidences to establish the truth and gravity of the issue
（事件分量）
. Though their topics have a local focus, news features should put everything together and show that there's larger context to the issue
（更广阔
社会、行业问题
关联）

shorter

Feature stories:

3.1 Body is the major area
When writing the body of a feature — which may include many short pieces — keep the following points in mind.
3.2 Never fill it, important part
T
he newspaper reading is a willing one, and if the reader feels that he is being bored or over-burdened, he might leave it even in the middle of the feature. So every word and concept must be rightly written and rightly placed.
3.3
W
ell-connected, forceful and coordinated
All the paragraphs of the main body should be well-connected with each other. Jumping from one idea to another should be well-thought and must be seen whether it is getting its connection to the previous or the following paragraphs. Never take it for granted that the reader is going to form linkages in the thought process.

===== SLIDE 54 (ppt/slides/slide54.xml) =====
Structure of a Feature
- Length
5 Length
If a reporter asks how long a feature story should be, the editor
may reply, 'as long as you keep it interesting.'  Feature  stories  vary in length from two or three paragraphs to 15 or 20 triple-spaced sheets of
copy. Readers' interest is  the  main  yardstick
by which they are judged. And editors are paid to accurately

Language features of news (
structure
)
The structure of a
News Report
follows a specific structure. The parts of this structure are identified and defined below:
Headline:
The Headline is the title of the article which is made up of 10 words or less. This title has two purposes: to introduce the
topic
of the article and to capture the
readers’ interest
, usually through controversial wording.

===== SLIDE 56 (ppt/slides/slide56.xml) =====
Subheading
（副标题）
:
The Subheading, which is not always included in an article, expands upon the Headline’s content, if needed.
By-line
（署名行）
:
The by-line includes the article’s author’s name, and sometimes that person’s occupation.

===== SLIDE 57 (ppt/slides/slide57.xml) =====
Summary Lead:
The Summary Lead consists of the first paragraph, the 1-2 sentences consisting of less than 30 words that begin an article. The content of this paragraph consists of the 5WH (who, what, where, when, why, and how) of the article.

===== SLIDE 58 (ppt/slides/slide58.xml) =====
Fact-based Paragraphs:
The remainder of the article consists of short paragraphs that expand on the 5WH mentioned in the Summary Lead and include quotations from key people involved in or related to the article. The content of these paragraphs is
fact-based
and organized from
most to least important
, so that the editor can cut the bottom paragraphs out if needed.
